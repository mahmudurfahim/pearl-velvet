About the studio

Started at a kitchen table, still run that way

Pearl Velvet began in 2021 in Dhaka as a way to make the jewellery we couldn't find — pieces built from two honest materials instead of a dozen cheap ones. Every design starts as a sketch in velvet and pearl before it's approved for a run.

We're a team of six: two jewellers, a stitcher who's worked in velvet for eighteen years, and three of us who pack every single order by hand. Nothing ships that we wouldn't wear ourselves.

— The Pearl Velvet studio

Good to know

Shipping, returns & FAQ

  • Choose Cash on Delivery at checkout — no card or online payment needed.
  • We confirm your order by phone or email within 24 hours.
  • Pay the courier in cash (or UPI, where the courier supports it) when your parcel arrives.
  • Dhaka: 1–3 business days.
  • Rest of Bangladesh: 3–6 business days.
  • Shipping is free on every order — no minimum spend, no exceptions.
  • Unworn pieces in original packaging can be returned within 7 days of delivery.
  • Earrings are final sale for hygiene reasons.
  • Email hello@pearlvelvet.example with your order number to start a return.
  • Wipe pearls with a soft dry cloth after wearing — avoid perfume and lotion contact.
  • Store velvet pieces flat, away from direct sunlight.
  • Spot clean velvet only; never machine wash.
